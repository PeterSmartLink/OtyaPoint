# Brand and product language

## Official hierarchy

- **Legal company:** Otya Hold Ltd
- **Trading name:** OmniPoint
- **Technology ecosystem:** Otya OS
- **Connected products:** Otya Auth, Otya Core, Otya Next, Otya Media, OmniPoint

## Approved usage

- Formal/legal context: “Otya Hold Ltd trading as OmniPoint” where the legal entity and trade name both need to be shown.
- Customer-facing physical shop and product: “OmniPoint”.
- Ecosystem overview: “Otya OS — one connected ecosystem.”
- Otya Auth: identity and access.
- Otya Core: shared APIs and common platform services.
- Otya Next: AI assistant and customer support.
- Otya Media: offline music and video player.
- OmniPoint: retail point of sale, inventory and mobile-money agency operations.

Do not use “OmniPoint UG Limited” as the legal entity name unless its separate registration is verified. “T/A” means “trading as”; it does not by itself create another legal entity.

## Voice and visual principles

- Professional, friendly, trustworthy, clear and locally understandable.
- Prefer everyday words, short instructions and helpful next steps.
- Use consistent names, typography, spacing, button labels, status colors and accessible contrast across products.
- Explain failures without blame; never imply a payment, refund, stock update or account action succeeded unless the system confirms it.
- Do not put secrets, real customer details, account credentials or sensitive financial information in screenshots, examples or public documentation.

## Change control

This document is the naming baseline for the OmniPoint repository. Changes to legal identity must be verified against official registration/tax documents; product naming changes should be coordinated across UI, API docs, repositories and support materials. Avoid destructive renames of existing resources merely to match naming.
